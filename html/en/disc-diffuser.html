Anti-Backflow Design

Self-Sealing Membrane Structure

The EV-270 does not rely on an additional external check valve. Instead, its membrane structure is designed to help reduce wastewater backflow when the air supply is interrupted.

When air pressure is supplied, the membrane opens and releases fine bubbles. When air supply stops, the elastic membrane closes against the diffuser body, helping prevent wastewater from entering the air piping system.

During Aeration Air pressure opens the membrane pores and releases fine bubbles
When Air Stops The elastic membrane returns to a closed position
Benefit Helps reduce wastewater backflow into the diffuser and piping system
Design Advantage No additional check valve component required

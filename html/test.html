Key Product Features

Designed for reliable wastewater aeration operation with practical engineering structure and stable air distribution performance.

產品特色

Spin-Welded Structure

Integrated air inlet with spin-welded connection helps improve sealing reliability and reduce side-leakage risk.

旋熔式結構

一體成型進氣口搭配旋熔接合，有助於提升密封可靠性並降低側漏風險。

EPDM Membrane

EPDM engineering rubber membrane designed for long-term wastewater aeration environments.

EPDM 膜片

採用 EPDM 工程橡膠膜片，適用於長時間污水曝氣環境。
